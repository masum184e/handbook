Team Reallocation

Team Reallocation is the process of reassigning or redistributing team members, roles, responsibilities, or resources from one project, task, or department to another in order to optimize project performance, balance workloads, meet deadlines, or respond to changing project requirements.

Team reallocation is often necessary when project priorities change, new skills are required, team members become unavailable, or schedules need adjustment.

Objectives of Team Reallocation​

The main objectives of team reallocation are to:

  • Improve resource utilization.
  • Meet project deadlines.
  • Balance team workload.
  • Address skill shortages.
  • Respond to changing project requirements.
  • Reduce project delays.
  • Increase productivity.
  • Improve project quality.

Reasons for Team Reallocation​

Team members may be reassigned for several reasons.

  1. Change in Project Priority
    • A high-priority project may require additional developers.
    • A company is developing both a Payroll System and an Online Banking System. Since the banking project has an earlier deadline, two developers are moved from the payroll project to the banking project.
  2. Skill Requirements
    • Certain project phases require specialized skills.
    • A cybersecurity expert is reassigned to perform security testing before software deployment.
  3. Resource Availability
    • If a team member becomes unavailable due to illness, resignation, or leave, another employee may replace them.
    • A senior backend developer resigns. Another experienced backend developer is assigned to continue the work.
  4. Workload Balancing
    • Some team members may be overloaded while others have fewer tasks.
    • One frontend developer has ten pending tasks, while another has only three. The project manager redistributes the work to balance the workload.
  5. Project Delays
    • Projects falling behind schedule may receive additional staff.
    • The testing phase is delayed, so two extra QA engineers are temporarily assigned to complete testing on time.

Example: Team Reallocation in an Online Shopping System​

Suppose a company is developing an Online Shopping System.

Initial Team​

Team MemberRole
AliceFrontend Developer
BobBackend Developer
CharlieDatabase Engineer
DavidQA Engineer
EmmaUI/UX Designer

Situation​

The Payment Module falls behind schedule because it is more complex than expected.

Team Reallocation​

The project manager decides to:

  • Move Alice to help with frontend payment integration.
  • Assign another experienced backend developer from a different project to assist Bob.
  • Add one more QA engineer for payment testing.

New Team Allocation​

Team MemberNew Responsibility
AlicePayment UI
BobPayment API
CharliePayment Database
DavidPayment Testing
New QA EngineerSecurity Testing
New Backend DeveloperPayment Integration

Explanation​

This reallocation:

  • Speeds up development.
  • Balances workload.
  • Reduces schedule delays.
  • Improves software quality by increasing testing capacity.

Advantages of Team Reallocation​

  • Better utilization of human resources.
  • Faster completion of critical tasks.
  • Improved productivity.
  • Better workload distribution.
  • Increased project flexibility.
  • Better response to unexpected changes.
  • Improved project quality.
  • Reduced project delays.
  • Enhanced collaboration across teams.

Disadvantages of Team Reallocation​

  • Learning curve for new team members.
  • Temporary productivity loss during transition.
  • Possible communication issues.
  • Reduced morale if employees are moved frequently.
  • Knowledge transfer may take time.
  • Other projects may experience resource shortages.

Difference Between Team Allocation and Team Reallocation​

Team AllocationTeam Reallocation
Initial assignment of team members to a project or tasks.Reassignment of team members after the project has started.
Done during project planning.Done during project execution or monitoring.
Based on the initial project plan.Based on changing requirements, risks, or priorities.
Focuses on creating the project team.Focuses on optimizing the existing team.

Real-World Scenario​

A software company is developing three applications simultaneously:

  1. Hospital Management System
  2. E-Commerce Platform
  3. School Management System

The E-Commerce Platform receives an urgent request from the client to launch before a major holiday shopping season. The project manager temporarily reallocates two experienced frontend developers and one QA engineer from the School Management System to the E-Commerce Platform. Their responsibilities include implementing the checkout process, optimizing the user interface, and performing intensive testing.

Outcome:

  • The E-Commerce Platform is completed on schedule and launched successfully before the holiday season.
  • The School Management System experiences a minor delay, but its adjusted timeline remains acceptable to the client.
  • Overall, the organization's resources are used more effectively by prioritizing the most time-sensitive project.
