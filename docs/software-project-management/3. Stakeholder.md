A **stakeholder** is **any individual, group, or organization that can influence, is influenced by, or has an interest in the software project and its outcomes**. Stakeholders may participate directly in the project, provide resources, make decisions, use the software, or be affected by its success or failure.

Stakeholders are important because they define project requirements, provide feedback, approve deliverables, allocate resources, and ensure the project aligns with business goals.

## Objectives of Stakeholder Management

The main objectives of stakeholder management are to:

* Identify all project stakeholders.
* Understand their needs and expectations.
* Maintain effective communication.
* Gain stakeholder support and commitment.
* Resolve conflicts among stakeholders.
* Ensure project success by satisfying stakeholder requirements.

## Types of Stakeholders

Stakeholders are generally classified into **Internal Stakeholders** and **External Stakeholders**.

### Internal Stakeholders

Internal stakeholders belong to the organization developing the software.

**Examples**

* Project Manager
* Software Developers
* Quality Assurance (QA) Engineers
* Business Analysts
* UI/UX Designers
* Database Administrators
* System Administrators
* Senior Management

Internal stakeholders are directly involved in planning, developing, testing, and managing the project.

### External Stakeholders

External stakeholders are outside the development organization but have an interest in the project.

**Examples**

* Clients
* Customers
* End Users
* Suppliers
* Government Agencies
* Investors
* Business Partners

External stakeholders influence project requirements, funding, legal compliance, and software acceptance.

## Major Stakeholders and Their Roles

### Project Sponsor

The project sponsor provides financial support and overall project direction.

**Responsibilities**

* Approves project budget.
* Provides resources.
* Makes high-level decisions.
* Reviews project progress.

**Example**

A bank funds the development of a mobile banking application.

### Project Manager

The project manager plans, executes, monitors, and controls the software project.

**Responsibilities**

* Prepare project plans.
* Manage schedule and budget.
* Allocate resources.
* Manage risks.
* Communicate with stakeholders.

**Example**

The project manager coordinates developers, testers, and clients to ensure timely delivery.

### Client

The client requests the software and defines business requirements.

**Responsibilities**

* Provide project requirements.
* Approve deliverables.
* Review project progress.
* Accept the final software.

**Example**

A hospital hires a software company to build a Hospital Management System.

### End Users

End users are the people who actually use the software.

**Responsibilities**

* Provide feedback.
* Participate in user acceptance testing (UAT).
* Suggest improvements.

**Example**

Doctors and nurses use a Hospital Management System daily.

## Example: Stakeholders in an Online Shopping System

Suppose a software company is developing an **Online Shopping System**.

| Stakeholder              | Role                  | Contribution                                                              |
| ------------------------ | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Client                   | Requests the system   | Defines business requirements and approves the final product.             |
| Project Manager          | Manages the project   | Plans schedules, budgets, resources, and risks.                           |
| Business Analyst         | Requirement gathering | Documents business needs and translates them into technical requirements. |
| Developers               | Build the software    | Develop frontend, backend, and database components.                       |
| QA Engineers             | Test the software     | Ensure the application works correctly and meets quality standards.       |
| UI/UX Designer           | Designs interfaces    | Creates user-friendly and visually appealing screens.                     |
| Customers                | End users             | Purchase products and provide feedback on usability.                      |
| Payment Gateway Provider | External service      | Processes secure online payments.                                         |

Each stakeholder contributes differently:

* The **client** defines what features the system should include.
* The **business analyst** converts business needs into detailed requirements.
* **Developers** implement the features.
* **QA engineers** test the system for bugs and performance.
* **Customers** use the application and provide feedback that helps improve future versions.
* The **payment gateway provider** ensures secure and reliable payment processing.

Because all stakeholders have different roles and expectations, effective communication and coordination are essential for project success.

## Advantages of Effective Stakeholder Management

* Clear project requirements.
* Better communication.
* Improved decision-making.
* Higher customer satisfaction.
* Reduced project risks.
* Faster conflict resolution.
* Increased stakeholder trust.
* Greater project success rate.

## Challenges in Stakeholder Management

* Conflicting stakeholder expectations.
* Frequent requirement changes.
* Poor communication.
* Resistance to change.
* Limited stakeholder availability.
* Differing priorities among stakeholders.


## Difference Between Stakeholders and Team Members

| Stakeholders                                                  | Team Members                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Anyone who has an interest in or is affected by the project.  | Individuals who directly perform project tasks.                               |
| Can be internal or external.                                  | Usually internal to the project team.                                         |
| May influence project decisions without doing technical work. | Responsible for executing project activities.                                 |
| Examples: Client, Sponsor, End Users, Government Agencies.    | Examples: Developers, QA Engineers, UI/UX Designers, Database Administrators. |
