Speaking Question

IELTS Speaking Part 1 Question Bank​

Hometown​

1. Where are you from?

Normal Band:

I’m from Jessore, which is a city in the southwestern part of Bangladesh. It’s a fairly peaceful place, and I’ve lived there for most of my life.

Band 9:

I’m from Jessore, a city in southwestern Bangladesh. It’s relatively peaceful compared with larger cities like Dhaka, and I’ve spent most of my life there, so I feel quite attached to it.

2. What do you like about your hometown?

Normal Band:

What I like most about Jessore is that it’s quiet and less crowded than Dhaka. People are generally friendly, and I really enjoy the relaxed lifestyle and greenery.

Band 9:

What I appreciate most about my hometown is the balance between convenience and tranquillity. It has most of the facilities I need, but it doesn’t have the overwhelming crowds and constant noise of a major city. I’m also particularly fond of its greenery and friendly community.

danger

convenience: সুবিধা

tranquillity, peace, quite, calm: শান্তিপূর্ণ অবস্থা

3. What is your hometown famous for?

Normal Band:

Jessore is quite famous for its date molasses, which is known as khejur gur in Bengali. It’s also well known for agriculture and producing different kinds of fruits and vegetables.

Band 9:

Jessore is particularly well known for its agricultural products, especially date molasses, or khejur gur. The region has a strong agricultural tradition, so farming and horticulture are an important part of its local identity.

4. Is your hometown a good place for young people?

Normal Band:

Yes, I think it’s a reasonably good place for young people. There are schools, colleges, restaurants and some entertainment facilities. However, there aren’t as many career opportunities as there are in bigger cities.

Band 9:

I’d say it has both advantages and drawbacks. On the one hand, it offers a peaceful environment, relatively affordable living and access to education and basic amenities. On the other hand, ambitious young people may find the career opportunities somewhat limited compared with larger cities.

danger

amenities: সুযোগ-সুবিধা

5. Has your hometown changed much in recent years?

Normal Band:

Yes, it has changed quite a lot. There are more modern buildings, shopping centres and better roads than before. However, traffic has also increased because the city has become more developed.

Band 9:

Yes, quite noticeably. There has been considerable development in terms of roads, commercial areas, housing and infrastructure. The city feels much more modern than it did when I was younger, although this development has also resulted in heavier traffic and greater pressure on public spaces.

6. Would you like to live in your hometown in the future?

Normal Band:

Yes, I would like to live there in the future, especially when I’m older. I enjoy the peaceful environment and being close to my family. However, I might live somewhere else for work.

Band 9:

Yes, I could definitely see myself moving back there at some point. I might prefer to live in a larger city while I’m building my career, but eventually I think I’d really value the slower pace of life in Jessore. Being closer to my family would also be an important factor.

7. What would you like to change about your hometown?

Normal Band:

I’d like to improve the public transport system and reduce traffic congestion. I also think there should be more parks and recreational areas, especially for young people.

Band 9:

If I could change a few things, I’d focus on improving public transport, managing traffic more effectively and creating more green spaces. I’d also like to see more opportunities for young people, particularly in technology and entrepreneurship, so they could build careers locally.

8. Do many tourists visit your hometown?

Normal Band:

Not really. Jessore doesn’t attract as many tourists as some of the major tourist destinations in Bangladesh. However, some people visit the city because of its cultural and historical attractions.

Band 9:

It doesn’t attract huge numbers of international tourists compared with Bangladesh’s major tourist destinations. However, it does receive domestic visitors and travellers who are interested in the region’s cultural and historical attractions. I actually think its relatively low tourist profile is part of its charm.

Band 9:

Normal Band:

Yes, I’d be open to moving, particularly if I found a good career opportunity elsewhere. I think living in a different city would give me the chance to experience a new lifestyle and become more independent. That said, I’d definitely want to maintain a strong connection with my family and hometown.

8. What kind of home would you like to have in the future?

Normal Band:

More importantly, I’d like it to be somewhere that offers both privacy and easy access to the city, so I wouldn’t have to sacrifice convenience for tranquillity.

Studies​

1. What do you study?

Normal Band:

I’m currently studying Computer Science at university. I’ve always been interested in technology, so I decided to choose this subject. Most of my courses are related to programming, software development and computer systems.

Band 9:

I’m currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science. The course covers a wide range of areas, including programming, software development, databases and computer systems. I find it particularly interesting because technology is constantly evolving, so there’s always something new to learn.

2. Why did you choose your subject?

Normal Band:

I chose Computer Science because I’ve always been interested in computers and technology. I also think it offers good career opportunities, so I felt it would be a practical choice for my future.

Band 9:

I chose Computer Science because I’ve always been fascinated by how technology works and how software can be used to solve real-world problems. Another factor was the wide range of career opportunities it offers. So, for me, it was a combination of personal interest and long-term career prospects.

3. What do you enjoy most about your studies?

Normal Band:

What I enjoy most is programming because I like solving problems and creating something from scratch. I also enjoy working on practical projects with my classmates.

Band 9:

What I enjoy most is the problem-solving aspect of my studies. I particularly enjoy programming because it requires logical thinking and allows me to turn an idea into something practical. I also find project-based work rewarding because I can apply what I’ve learned rather than simply memorising information.

4. What is the most difficult part of your studies?

Normal Band:

The most difficult part is probably understanding some of the more complicated programming concepts. Sometimes I have to spend a lot of time practising before I fully understand them. Managing several assignments at the same time can also be stressful.

Band 9:

I’d say the most challenging aspect is dealing with complex concepts that require a great deal of logical reasoning. Some topics can take quite a while to fully grasp, particularly when they involve complicated algorithms or programming techniques. On top of that, balancing multiple deadlines can be quite demanding.

5. Do you prefer studying alone or with other people?

Normal Band:

I generally prefer studying alone because I can concentrate better and study at my own pace. However, I sometimes study with my classmates when I’m working on a difficult project or preparing for an exam.

Band 9:

For the most part, I prefer studying independently because I can work at my own pace and concentrate without getting distracted. Having said that, I do find group study useful for certain subjects, especially when I need to discuss a difficult concept or exchange ideas with my classmates.

6. What kind of assignments do you usually have?

Normal Band:

We usually have programming tasks, written reports and practical projects. Sometimes we also have presentations and group assignments. The workload can be quite heavy around exam time.

Band 9:

We’re generally given a mixture of theoretical and practical assignments. These include programming projects, technical reports, presentations and group-based tasks. I particularly enjoy practical projects because they give me an opportunity to apply theoretical concepts to real-world problems.

7. What subject would you like to study in the future?

Normal Band:

I’d like to study Artificial Intelligence in the future. I think it’s a fascinating field, and it has become increasingly important in many different industries. I’d like to learn more about how AI systems are developed and used.

Band 9:

I’d be particularly interested in studying Artificial Intelligence in greater depth. It’s a rapidly developing field with applications in almost every industry, and I find the idea of machines being able to learn and solve complex problems fascinating. I’d like to explore areas such as machine learning and natural language processing.

8. Do you think your studies will help you in your future career?

Normal Band:

Yes, definitely. My studies are helping me develop technical and problem-solving skills that I’ll need in the workplace. They’re also giving me practical experience through different projects, which should be useful when I start my career.

Band 9:

Absolutely. I think my degree will provide me with both the technical knowledge and problem-solving abilities I’ll need in my professional life. More importantly, the practical projects and teamwork involved in my course are helping me develop transferable skills, such as communication, time management and collaboration, which should be valuable regardless of the specific career path I eventually pursue.
