IDIOMS

Introduction — WHAT​

No.Phrase / IdiomCategoryMeaningIELTS Example
1I’d like to talk about…Introducing a topicIntroduce a topicI’d like to talk about a trip I took with my friends.
2One thing I’d like to mention is…Introducing a topicIntroduce a specific pointOne thing I’d like to mention is my first laptop.
3The first thing that comes to mind is…Recalling a memoryThe first thing you rememberThe first thing that comes to mind is my graduation ceremony.
4What immediately comes to mind is…Recalling a memorySomething you instantly rememberWhat immediately comes to mind is a teacher who inspired me.
5A memorable experienceDescribing an experienceAn unforgettable experienceIt was one of the most memorable experiences of my life.
6A remarkable experienceDescribing an experienceAn unusually good or important experienceIt was a truly remarkable experience for me.
7It has always stood out to me.Expressing lasting memoriesSomething that remains memorableThat particular experience has always stood out to me.
8Something close to my heartExpressing emotional attachmentSomething emotionally importantMy childhood home is something very close to my heart.
  • Both “comes to mind” and “comes to my mind” are grammatically possible, but “comes to mind” is more natural and common in English, especially in IELTS Speaking.

I’d like to talk about a trip I took with my friends a few years ago. The first thing that comes to mind is the beautiful scenery and the wonderful time we spent together. What immediately comes to mind is the breathtaking view of the mountains, which made the trip truly special. One thing I’d like to mention is that we had a lot of fun exploring new places and trying local food. It was one of the most memorable experiences of my life and, in many ways, a remarkable experience because it brought us closer together. That particular trip has always stood out to me because it gave me so many happy memories. Even today, those moments are something close to my heart.

Background — WHEN + WHERE​

WHEN​

No.Phrase / IdiomCategoryMeaningIELTS Example
1Back in the dayReferring to the pastIn the past, especially when remembering earlier timesBack in the day, I used to play football every afternoon.
2When I was in schoolReferring to a life stageDuring my school yearsWhen I was in school, I was extremely interested in computers.
3During my university daysReferring to a life stageDuring my university lifeDuring my university days, I became interested in artificial intelligence.
4At that point in my lifeReferring to a particular periodAt a specific stage of my lifeAt that point in my life, I was preparing for university.
5At the timeReferring to a past situationDuring that particular periodAt the time, I didn't realize how important the experience would become.
6It was around the time when…Describing approximate timingApproximately when something happenedIt was around the time when I was finishing my bachelor's degree.
7A few years agoReferring to the distant pastSeveral years before nowA few years ago, I visited Cox's Bazar with my family.
8Not too long agoReferring to the recent pastFairly recentlyNot too long ago, I attended a technology conference.
9Earlier this yearReferring to a specific timeAt an earlier point in the current yearEarlier this year, I started preparing for the IELTS.

I’d like to talk about a skill I learned during my university days, which was programming. Back in the day, when I was in school, I was extremely interested in computers, but I didn't know much about how they worked. At that point in my life, I was eager to explore the world of technology. It was around the time when I started my university studies that I became interested in artificial intelligence. At the time, I found programming quite challenging, but I gradually improved through regular practice. A few years ago, I never imagined that I would become so passionate about this field. Not too long ago, I also started learning some advanced programming techniques. Earlier this year, I decided to improve my technical skills further, and that decision has motivated me to keep learning.

WHERE​

No.Phrase / IdiomCategoryMeaningIELTS Example
1It took place in…Describing where an event happenedHappened in a placeIt took place in my hometown.
2It happened at…Describing a specific locationHappened at a particular placeIt happened at my university.
3I first came across it in…Describing where you discovered somethingFirst encountered somethingI first came across this book in a small bookstore.
4Not far from where I liveDescribing proximityClose to my homeThere's a beautiful park not far from where I live.
5In the heart of…Describing a central locationIn the central part of a placeThe hotel was located in the heart of the city.
6A stone's throw from…Describing proximityVery close to somethingMy university is just a stone's throw from my home.

I'd like to talk about a beautiful park in my hometown. It is located in the heart of the city, not far from where I live. In fact, it's just a stone's throw from my home, so I can easily walk there whenever I have free time. I first came across it when I was in school, and I've been visiting it ever since. A memorable event I attended there took place in the winter, and it happened at the park's main entrance. Overall, it's one of my favourite places because it's peaceful and convenient to visit.

Details — HOW​

Phrase / IdiomMeaningIELTS Example
Basically,…Introduces basic explanationBasically, we decided to travel without making a detailed plan.
To give you a bit of background…Provide contextTo give you a bit of background, I had never travelled alone before.
What happened was…Explain an eventWhat happened was that our train was delayed by three hours.
The whole thing started when…Explain beginningThe whole thing started when my friend suggested going on a trip.
Initially,…At the beginningInitially, I wasn't interested in joining them.
All of a sudden…Very suddenlyAll of a sudden, it started raining heavily.
One thing led to another.One event caused anotherOne thing led to another, and eventually we decided to start a business together.
As time went by…As time passedAs time went by, I became more confident.
Eventually…Finally, after some timeEventually, we managed to reach our destination.
In the end…FinallyIn the end, everything worked out perfectly.
What really caught my attention was…What attracted your attentionWhat really caught my attention was the beautiful architecture.

Very useful sequencing pattern​

Initially → Then → After that → All of a sudden → Eventually → In the end

Example:

Initially, we were a little nervous. Then we started talking to other people. After that, we explored the city. All of a sudden, it started raining. Eventually, we found a café, and in the end, we had a wonderful time.

The most useful 30 to memorize​

If you're currently building your IELTS vocabulary, don't try to memorize all of them at once. Start with these:

Introduction​

  1. The first thing that comes to mind is…
  2. I'd like to talk about…
  3. It has always stood out to me.

Background​

  1. Back in the day…
  2. At that point in my life…
  3. It took place in…
  4. A stone's throw from…

Details​

  1. Basically,…
  2. What happened was…
  3. Initially,…
  4. All of a sudden…
  5. One thing led to another.
  6. Eventually,…

Example​

  1. For instance,…
  2. To give you an example…
  3. Take ___, for example.
  4. A case in point is…

Why​

  1. What makes it special is…
  2. It means a lot to me because…
  3. It had a significant impact on me.
  4. It was a turning point in my life.
  5. It broadened my horizons.

Feelings​

  1. I was over the moon.
  2. It made my day.
  3. I was pleasantly surprised.
  4. I felt a huge sense of achievement.
  5. It really touched me.
  6. It left a lasting impression on me.

Conclusion​

  1. Looking back,…
  2. All in all,…

One complete mini-example​

What: The first thing that comes to mind is my graduation ceremony. When: It happened recently, when I was finishing my bachelor's degree. Where: It took place at my university. How: Initially, I was quite nervous, but eventually I started enjoying the whole experience. Example: To give you an example, I remember taking pictures with all my closest friends. Why: What made it special was that it marked a turning point in my life. Feeling: I was absolutely over the moon and felt a huge sense of achievement. Conclusion: Looking back, it left a lasting impression on me, and it's definitely something I'll never forget.
