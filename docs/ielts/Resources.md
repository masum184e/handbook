## Detailed Breakdown

### 🥇 1. IELTS.org — Official IELTS

**Best for:** Understanding the actual IELTS exam.

This should be your **first source**, because it is the official IELTS website.

You can find:

- Listening practice
- Reading practice
- Writing samples
- Speaking samples
- Sample questions
- Test format information
- Assessment information
- Academic and General Training resources

**Why use it:**
When you're unsure whether a third-party website represents the actual IELTS format correctly, use IELTS.org as the reference.

### 🥇 2. British Council IELTS

**Best for:** Full practice and realistic exam preparation.

The British Council provides practice resources for:

- Listening
- Reading
- Writing
- Speaking
- Computer-delivered IELTS
- Practice tests
- Preparation courses/resources

It is especially useful for getting comfortable with the **computer-delivered test experience**.

### 🥇 3. IDP IELTS

**Best for:** Computer-delivered IELTS preparation.

IDP is one of the organizations that administers IELTS, so its preparation materials are highly relevant.

Useful for:

- Listening
- Reading
- Writing
- Speaking
- Computer IELTS
- Mock tests
- Exam preparation

### 🥇 4. Cambridge IELTS

**Best for:** Authentic exam-style questions.

Cambridge IELTS materials are particularly valuable because they closely reflect the style and difficulty of actual IELTS tests.

For a **Band 8–9 target**, I would prioritize Cambridge practice tests over random third-party tests.

Use them for:

- Full Listening tests
- Full Reading tests
- Writing Task 1
- Writing Task 2
- Speaking practice

### ⭐ 5. Jump Into

**Best for:** General IELTS preparation.

It can be useful as an additional source for:

- Practice
- Preparation resources
- Test-taking strategies
- IELTS materials

### ⭐ 6. IELTS Online Tests

**Best for:** Doing a **large number of online mock tests**.

[IELTS Online Tests](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/) has a very large collection of IELTS practice material.

You can find:

- Listening tests
- Reading tests
- Writing tests
- Speaking practice
- Full mock tests
- Practice by question type
- Computer-style testing
- AI-related evaluation features

**Why I like it**

After you've completed the official Cambridge material, you need **more practice**. This is where IELTS Online Tests becomes useful.

### ⭐ 7. Mini IELTS

**Best for:** Huge amounts of **<span className="danger">Reading and Listening practice</span>**.

One of its biggest advantages is practicing individual question types.

For example:

#### Listening

- Multiple Choice
- Matching
- Form Completion
- Note Completion
- Sentence Completion
- Table Completion
- Flow-chart Completion
- Plan/Map/Diagram Labelling

#### Reading

- Multiple Choice
- True/False/Not Given
- Yes/No/Not Given
- Matching Headings
- Matching Information
- Sentence Completion
- Summary Completion
- Diagram Labelling

This makes it particularly useful when you have identified a **specific weak question type**.

### ⭐ 8. IELTS Training Online

**Best for:** Additional practice material.

It has a large collection of:

- Listening tests
- Reading tests
- Writing topics
- Speaking topics
- Vocabulary
- Grammar
- Practice exercises
- Mock tests

I would use it primarily as a **supplementary practice library**.

### ⭐ 9. IELTS Writing Info

**Best for:** IELTS <span className="danger">Writing practice</span>.

Useful if you're specifically working on:

- Writing Task 1
- Writing Task 2
- Essay topics
- Sample questions
- Exam-style writing practice

However, for **band evaluation**, don't rely solely on the website. Compare your writing against official band descriptors and high-quality examiner/sample answers.

### ⭐ 10. Magoosh IELTS

**Best for:** Learning + practice + explanations.

Magoosh is particularly useful when you don't just want to **do questions**, but also want to understand:

:::danger

> "Why is this answer correct?"

:::

It offers:

- Lessons
- Practice questions
- Vocabulary
- Strategy
- Explanations
- Writing/speaking resources
- Paid/AI features

### ⭐ 11. Alfa IELTS

**Best for:** AI-powered IELTS practice.

Alfa IELTS provides:

- Full mock tests
- Section-specific practice
- Listening
- Reading
- Writing
- Speaking
- AI-based evaluation
- Timed practice

The AI evaluation can be useful for getting additional feedback, although you shouldn't assume an AI-generated band score is identical to an official examiner's score.

## 🎯 How I would use them for Band 8–9

Don't try to use all 11 websites equally. That will create a lot of unnecessary practice.

I'd use this hierarchy:

### Level 1: Authenticity

- IELTS.org → British Council → IDP → Cambridge
- Use these to understand the real IELTS exam.

### Level 2: Lots of practice

- IELTS Online Tests → Mini IELTS → Alfa IELTS
- Use these when you need more tests after completing official material.

### Level 3: Targeted improvement

- Magoosh → IELTS Training Online → IELTS Writing Info → Jump Into
- Use these for additional lessons, strategies, question practice, and weaknesses.

<summary>What are the role of British Council, IDP, and Cambridge English for IELTS test exam</summary>

The British Council, IDP, and Cambridge English are the three main organizations associated with IELTS. They have different roles, but all three are connected to the same English-language test.

**British Council**

The British Council is a UK organization that promotes education and cultural relations internationally. In IELTS, it is one of the organizations that administers the test.

- Offers IELTS test registration and test-day arrangements in many countries.
- Provides preparation materials and English-learning resources.
- Delivers IELTS tests through authorized test centres.

**IDP**

IDP is an international education organization(from Australia) and an IELTS co-owner.

- Organizes IELTS test registration and delivery.
- Provides IELTS preparation resources.
- Also offers international education and study-abroad services in many countries.

**Cambridge English**

Cambridge English is part of Cambridge University Press & Assessment. Its key role in IELTS is developing and maintaining the test's English-language assessment standards.

- Contributes to IELTS test design and question development.
- Helps ensure that the test assesses English proficiency reliably.
- Supports the research and assessment standards behind IELTS.

**Are their IELTS exams different?**

No. IELTS is jointly owned by all three organizations. The test assesses the same four skills: Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking

| Feature                  | British Council                      | IDP                                  | Cambridge English                            |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Owns IELTS               | Yes                                  | Yes                                  | Yes                                          |
| Offers test registration | Yes                                  | Yes                                  | Not typically as a separate booking provider |
| Develops test materials  | Contributes to the IELTS partnership | Contributes to the IELTS partnership | Major role in test development               |
| Main role                | Test delivery and education          | Test delivery and education services | Assessment expertise and test development    |

The main difference between British Council and IDP for IELTS is the organization that manages your booking and test arrangements. Both are official IELTS test providers, and neither is inherently better for getting a higher band score.

**Which one should you choose?**

If you're taking IELTS in Bangladesh, you can compare the available test dates, test centre location, registration arrangements, and preparation resources offered by British Council and IDP. Your IELTS score is not supposed to be higher simply because you choose one provider over the other.

One important point: Cambridge English also publishes official IELTS preparation books and practice tests, so you can use its materials regardless of which provider you book with.
