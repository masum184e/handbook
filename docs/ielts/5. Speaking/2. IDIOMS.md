## Introduction — WHAT

| No. | Phrase / Idiom | Category | Meaning | IELTS Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | I’d like to talk about… | Introducing a topic | Introduce a topic | I’d like to talk about a trip I took with my friends. |
| 2 | One thing I’d like to mention is… | Introducing a topic | Introduce a specific point | One thing I’d like to mention is my first laptop. |
| 3 | The first thing that comes to mind is… | Recalling a memory | The first thing you remember | The first thing that comes to mind is my graduation ceremony. |
| 4 | What immediately comes to mind is… | Recalling a memory | Something you instantly remember | What immediately comes to mind is a teacher who inspired me. |
| 5 | A memorable experience | Describing an experience | An unforgettable experience | It was one of the most memorable experiences of my life. |
| 6 | A remarkable experience | Describing an experience | An unusually good or important experience | It was a truly remarkable experience for me. |
| 7 | It has always stood out to me. | Expressing lasting memories | Something that remains memorable | That particular experience has always stood out to me. |
| 8 | Something close to my heart | Expressing emotional attachment | Something emotionally important | My childhood home is something very close to my heart. |

- Both “comes to mind” and “comes to my mind” are grammatically possible, but “comes to mind” is more natural and common in English, especially in IELTS Speaking.

> I’d like to talk about a trip I took with my friends a few years ago. The first thing that comes to mind is the beautiful scenery and the wonderful time we spent together. What immediately comes to mind is the breathtaking view of the mountains, which made the trip truly special. One thing I’d like to mention is that we had a lot of fun exploring new places and trying local food. It was one of the most memorable experiences of my life and, in many ways, a remarkable experience because it brought us closer together. That particular trip has always stood out to me because it gave me so many happy memories. Even today, those moments are something close to my heart.
## Background — WHEN + WHERE

### WHEN

| No. | Phrase / Idiom | Category | Meaning | IELTS Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Back in the day | Referring to the past | In the past, especially when remembering earlier times | Back in the day, I used to play football every afternoon. |
| 2 | When I was in school | Referring to a life stage | During my school years | When I was in school, I was extremely interested in computers. |
| 3 | During my university days | Referring to a life stage | During my university life | During my university days, I became interested in artificial intelligence. |
| 4 | At that point in my life | Referring to a particular period | At a specific stage of my life | At that point in my life, I was preparing for university. |
| 5 | At the time | Referring to a past situation | During that particular period | At the time, I didn't realize how important the experience would become. |
| 6 | It was around the time when… | Describing approximate timing | Approximately when something happened | It was around the time when I was finishing my bachelor's degree. |
| 7 | A few years ago | Referring to the distant past | Several years before now | A few years ago, I visited Cox's Bazar with my family. |
| 8 | Not too long ago | Referring to the recent past | Fairly recently | Not too long ago, I attended a technology conference. |
| 9 | Earlier this year | Referring to a specific time | At an earlier point in the current year | Earlier this year, I started preparing for the IELTS. |

> I’d like to talk about a skill I learned during my university days, which was programming. Back in the day, when I was in school, I was extremely interested in computers, but I didn't know much about how they worked. At that point in my life, I was eager to explore the world of technology. It was around the time when I started my university studies that I became interested in artificial intelligence. At the time, I found programming quite challenging, but I gradually improved through regular practice. A few years ago, I never imagined that I would become so passionate about this field. Not too long ago, I also started learning some advanced programming techniques. Earlier this year, I decided to improve my technical skills further, and that decision has motivated me to keep learning.

### WHERE

| No. | Phrase / Idiom | Category | Meaning | IELTS Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | It took place in… | Describing where an event happened | Happened in a place | It took place in my hometown. |
| 2 | It happened at… | Describing a specific location | Happened at a particular place | It happened at my university. |
| 3 | I first came across it in… | Describing where you discovered something | First encountered something | I first came across this book in a small bookstore. |
| 4 | Not far from where I live | Describing proximity | Close to my home | There's a beautiful park not far from where I live. |
| 5 | In the heart of… | Describing a central location | In the central part of a place | The hotel was located in the heart of the city. |
| 6 | A stone's throw from… | Describing proximity | Very close to something | My university is just a stone's throw from my home. |

> I'd like to talk about a beautiful park in my hometown. It is located in the heart of the city, not far from where I live. In fact, it's just a stone's throw from my home, so I can easily walk there whenever I have free time. I first came across it when I was in school, and I've been visiting it ever since. A memorable event I attended there took place in the winter, and it happened at the park's main entrance. Overall, it's one of my favourite places because it's peaceful and convenient to visit.

## Details — HOW

| Phrase / Idiom                           | Meaning                       | IELTS Example                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Basically,…                          | Introduces basic explanation  | *Basically, we decided to travel without making a detailed plan.*                   |
| To give you a bit of background…     | Provide context               | *To give you a bit of background, I had never travelled alone before.*              |
| What happened was…                   | Explain an event              | *What happened was that our train was delayed by three hours.*                      |
| The whole thing started when…        | Explain beginning             | *The whole thing started when my friend suggested going on a trip.*                 |
| Initially,…                          | At the beginning              | *Initially, I wasn't interested in joining them.*                                   |
| All of a sudden…                     | Very suddenly                 | *All of a sudden, it started raining heavily.*                                      |
| One thing led to another.            | One event caused another      | *One thing led to another, and eventually we decided to start a business together.* |
| As time went by…                     | As time passed                | *As time went by, I became more confident.*                                         |
| Eventually…                          | Finally, after some time      | *Eventually, we managed to reach our destination.*                                  |
| In the end…                          | Finally                       | *In the end, everything worked out perfectly.*                                      |
| What really caught my attention was… | What attracted your attention | *What really caught my attention was the beautiful architecture.*                   |

### Very useful sequencing pattern

> Initially → Then → After that → All of a sudden → Eventually → In the end

Example:

> *Initially, we were a little nervous. Then we started talking to other people. After that, we explored the city. All of a sudden, it started raining. Eventually, we found a café, and in the end, we had a wonderful time.*

If you're currently building your IELTS vocabulary, don't try to memorize all of them at once. Start with these:

1. The first thing that comes to mind is…
2. I'd like to talk about…
3. It has always stood out to me.

5. At that point in my life…

### Example

### One complete mini-example

> What: *The first thing that comes to mind is my graduation ceremony.*
> When: *It happened recently, when I was finishing my bachelor's degree.*
> Where: *It took place at my university.*
> How: *Initially, I was quite nervous, but eventually I started enjoying the whole experience.*
> Example: *To give you an example, I remember taking pictures with all my closest friends.*
> Why: *What made it special was that it marked a turning point in my life.*
> Feeling: *I was absolutely over the moon and felt a huge sense of achievement.*
> Conclusion: *Looking back, it left a lasting impression on me, and it's definitely something I'll never forget.*
