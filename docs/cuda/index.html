If by “NVIDIA tech” you mean major technologies and platforms developed by NVIDIA (NVIDIA), here are some of the most important ones:

AI & Machine Learning​

  • CUDA — Platform and programming model for GPU computing.
  • TensorRT — Optimizes and accelerates AI inference.
  • NVIDIA NeMo — Framework for building and customizing large language models.
  • NVIDIA NIM — Deployable AI model services.
  • NVIDIA DGX Software — Enterprise AI training and deployment tools.

Graphics & Gaming​

  • DLSS — AI-powered upscaling and frame generation.
  • NVIDIA Reflex — Reduces system latency in games.
  • RTX — Ray tracing and AI graphics technologies.
  • RTX Remix — AI-assisted remastering of classic games.
  • NVIDIA Broadcast — AI noise removal, background effects, and webcam enhancements.

Data Center & Cloud​

  • NVIDIA DGX — AI training servers.
  • NVIDIA HGX — High-performance AI infrastructure.
  • NVIDIA AI Enterprise — AI tools and support for businesses.
  • NVIDIA Omniverse Cloud — Cloud services for simulation and digital twins.

Simulation & Robotics​

  • NVIDIA Omniverse — Real-time simulation and digital twin creation.
  • NVIDIA Isaac — Robotics AI and simulation tools.
  • NVIDIA Cosmos — Models for robotics and simulation applications.

Automotive​

  • NVIDIA DRIVE — Self-driving and in-vehicle AI technology.
  • DRIVE Orin — Vehicle computing platform.
  • DRIVE Thor — Advanced autonomous-driving processor.

Hardware Architectures​

  • Blackwell
  • Hopper
  • Ada Lovelace
  • Ampere
