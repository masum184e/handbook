Segmentation

Image segmentation is the process of dividing an image into multiple meaningful and non-overlapping regions or objects so that each region contains pixels with similar characteristics such as color, intensity, texture, or shape.

In simple words:

Image Segmentation = Partitioning an image into meaningful regions or objects.

For example, in a medical image, segmentation separates organs or tumors from the surrounding tissues. In autonomous vehicles, segmentation identifies roads, pedestrians, vehicles, and traffic signs.

Objectives of Image Segmentation​

The main objectives are:

  • Separate objects from the background.
  • Identify regions of interest (ROI).
  • Simplify image representation.
  • Improve object recognition and classification.
  • Reduce computational complexity for later processing.

Role in Pattern Recognition​

Pattern recognition aims to identify and classify objects within an image.

Image segmentation acts as a preprocessing step:

Input Image
      ↓
Image Enhancement
      ↓
Image Segmentation      ↓
Feature Extraction
      ↓
Pattern Recognition / Classification
      ↓
Decision Making

Without segmentation, features may be extracted from irrelevant background regions, reducing recognition accuracy.

Characteristics of Good Segmentation​

A good segmentation should satisfy the following:

  • High similarity within each region.
  • High difference between neighboring regions.
  • Closed boundaries.
  • No missing object parts.
  • Minimal over-segmentation or under-segmentation.

Types of Image Segmentation​

Threshold-Based Segmentation​

This is the simplest method.

Pixels are divided according to intensity values.

Principle

If pixel intensity > threshold → Object

Otherwise → Background

Region-Based Segmentation​

Pixels with similar properties are grouped together.

Two common techniques:

  • Region Growing
  • Region Splitting and Merging

Region Growing​

Steps

  1. Choose a seed pixel.
  2. Check neighboring pixels.
  3. Add similar pixels.
  4. Continue until no similar pixels remain.

Example

Image

10 11 12 82
10 10 13 83
90 91 92 95

Seed = pixel value 10

Segment becomes

10 10 10
10 11 12
10 10 13

Advantages

  • Produces connected regions

Disadvantages

  • Seed selection is important

Clustering-Based Segmentation​

Pixels are grouped using machine learning.

Common algorithm

  • K-Means Clustering

Algorithm

  1. Choose K clusters.
  2. Initialize cluster centers.
  3. Assign pixels to nearest cluster.
  4. Update centers.
  5. Repeat until convergence.

Watershed Segmentation​

Treats the image like a topographic surface.

  • Bright pixels → Hills
  • Dark pixels → Valleys

Water fills valleys.

Meeting points become segmentation boundaries.

Used in

  • Cell segmentation
  • Medical imaging

Deep Learning-Based Segmentation​

Modern systems use neural networks.

Popular models

  • U-Net
  • Mask R-CNN
  • DeepLab
  • SegNet

Advantages

  • Very accurate
  • Learns complex features

Disadvantages

  • Requires large datasets
  • Computationally expensive

Applications of Image Segmentation​

Image segmentation is widely used in many fields:

  1. Medical Imaging

    • Tumor detection
    • Organ segmentation
    • Blood vessel extraction

  2. Autonomous Vehicles

    • Lane detection
    • Pedestrian detection
    • Vehicle identification

  3. Satellite Imaging

    • Land cover classification
    • Forest monitoring
    • Urban planning

  4. Face Recognition

    • Face extraction
    • Eye detection
    • Skin segmentation

  5. Industrial Inspection

    • Crack detection
    • Surface defect detection
    • Quality control

  6. Agriculture

    • Crop monitoring
    • Disease detection
    • Weed identification

Advantages​

  • Simplifies image analysis.
  • Improves object detection and recognition.
  • Reduces processing time for later stages.
  • Enables accurate feature extraction.
  • Essential for medical, industrial, and computer vision applications.

Disadvantages​

  • Performance depends on image quality.
  • Noise can reduce segmentation accuracy.
  • Complex scenes may require advanced algorithms.
  • Deep learning methods require large labeled datasets and high computational resources.3.b
